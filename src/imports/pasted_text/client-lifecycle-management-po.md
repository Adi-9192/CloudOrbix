Design a modern enterprise-grade internal web application called “Client Lifecycle Management Portal (CLMP)” with a premium corporate dashboard experience. Follow a Microsoft Fluent Design and modern SaaS analytics style with a strong primary color theme of #1E40AF, complemented by white, light gray, and subtle blue accents. The UI should feel similar to a large-scale enterprise operations platform such as CloudAtlas, ServiceNow, Azure Portal, or Power BI dashboards. Use rounded cards, soft shadows, professional typography, executive-friendly layouts, rich data visualizations, responsive design, and high information density without feeling cluttered.

The portal is designed for Operations Teams, Delivery Managers, Service Managers, and Leadership to manage the complete lifecycle of clients from onboarding to offboarding, track service adoption, upload and analyze Excel data, monitor trends over 10+ years, and generate executive reports.

Include Microsoft Entra ID (Azure AD) SSO login page with a corporate split-screen design. The left section should contain cloud infrastructure, digital transformation, analytics, and onboarding/offboarding illustrations. The right section should contain a modern SSO sign-in card with “Sign in with Company Account”, support contact information, application version, and corporate branding.
